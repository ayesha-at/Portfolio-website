EchoesLab

Java · Fabric · Minecraft mod

A Minecraft mod that records everything you do — walking, mining, placing blocks — and lets you summon up to three ghost "Echoes" that play it back on loop, striding forward each time so a mined tunnel keeps extending on its own. Built against a moving target, since Minecraft's rendering and mapping systems changed mid-project.

Echo Labs

Vanilla JS · HTML5 Canvas

A browser puzzle platformer where your only tool is your own past — record a short run, then work alongside a looping echo of yourself to reach places one player can't. 13 modules, no frameworks, built from scratch.

Play demo View repo
